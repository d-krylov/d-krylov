## About

Hi there 👋 

I am C++ developer interested in graphics and low-level programming.
I like working close to the metal and building things from scratch: ray tracing, procedural generation, geometry processing, and small ShaderToy demos.

I'm also really into mathematics - I enjoy exploring different areas of math and solving olympiad problems. 
I also experiment with formal proof assistants such as Rocq and Lean.

Outside of programming and math: climbing, depressive music, and poetry.
